# China Rose SEO, Local Search, and AI Search Research Report

Research date: 2026-08-22  
Site audited: https://chinarosesa.com/  
Repository audited: `/home/wslunusn/CR-Website`

## 1. Executive Summary

China Rose has a fast, crawlable static website with clear primary conversion paths on the homepage: pickup, delivery, phone calls, directions, menu views, and location selection. The main SEO gap is not technical crawlability of the homepage; it is incomplete entity clarity. Search engines and AI systems can see the name, two addresses, phone numbers, order links, and a menu, but the site does not currently publish canonical URLs, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, Open Graph metadata, structured data, location-specific restaurant schema, or robust visible location copy.

The highest-confidence work is:

1. Add SEO infrastructure: canonical URLs, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, Open Graph basics, and noindex for internal/order utility pages where appropriate.
2. Add structured data for the China Rose brand and each restaurant location using only verified NAP, hours, cuisine, order/menu URLs, and map URLs.
3. Strengthen homepage and location pages with concise, factual copy that answers "what, where, how to order, hours, services" without keyword stuffing.
4. Resolve owner-only business facts before writing richer restaurant/about copy.
5. Clean up off-site NAP/hours inconsistency across Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Toast, Uber Eats, Waze, Yelp/TripAdvisor/directories, and local restaurant sites.

Current blockers:

- `docs/seo/STRATEGY.md` and `docs/seo/BUSINESS_FACTS.md` are referenced in `AGENTS.md` but are absent in this checkout.
- `npm run seo:check` is required by `AGENTS.md` but no `seo:check` script exists in `package.json`.
- Owner confirmation is needed for official hours, dine-in/takeout/delivery attributes, accepted ordering providers, location-specific facts, business history, photos, and preferred GBP categories.

Use JSON-LD with stable `@id` values:

- Brand/org: `https://chinarosesa.com/#organization`
- Website: `https://chinarosesa.com/#website`
- W Military restaurant: `https://chinarosesa.com/locations/w-military#restaurant`
- SW Military restaurant: `https://chinarosesa.com/locations/sw-military#restaurant`
- Menu: `https://chinarosesa.com/menu#menu`

Recommended placement:

- Homepage: `Organization`, `WebSite`, and an `ItemList` or `department` relationship connecting both location entities.
- Each location page: `Restaurant` with `name`, `address`, `telephone`, `url`, `menu`, `servesCuisine`, `openingHoursSpecification` after hours are confirmed, `hasMap`, `potentialAction` or `sameAs`/`url` links only where appropriate.
- Menu page: `Menu` only if the implementation can represent visible categories/items accurately; otherwise use `hasMenu` URLs from restaurant schema and keep visible text menu crawlable.
- Breadcrumbs: Add `BreadcrumbList` for `/menu` and each location page.

## 11. AI / LLM / GEO Analysis

Current official guidance does not support a separate "GEO hack" layer. Google states AI Overviews and AI Mode rely on existing Search fundamentals, crawlability, indexability, helpful content, internal links, visible text, page experience, structured data matching visible content, and up-to-date Business Profile information. Google also says no additional machine-readable AI text files or special AI schema are required.

Bing Webmaster Guidelines state that Bing and Copilot rely on crawling, indexing, URL consolidation, content clarity, authority, and trust signals, and warn against prompt-injection or AI-manipulation content.

OpenAI crawler policy should separate search visibility and training:

- `OAI-SearchBot` is for ChatGPT Search discovery.
- `GPTBot` is for content that may be used to train foundation models.
- `ChatGPT-User` is user-initiated and not used for automatic search crawling.

Recommended robots direction for China Rose:

- Allow public pages for major search crawlers and OAI-SearchBot.
- Ask owner whether to allow or disallow GPTBot for training.
- No `llms.txt` solely for AI SEO.
- No prompt-injection text.
- Ensure important facts are visible in normal HTML and structured data.

AI-answer gaps today:

| What is China Rose in San Antonio? | Mostly answerable, but thin. |
| Where are its locations? | Answerable. |

## 18. Risks And Anti-Patterns To Avoid

- Do not invent history, ownership, awards, signature dishes, local landmarks, or popularity.
- Do not add review schema from Google/Yelp/third-party reviews.
- Do not create doorway pages by ZIP, neighborhood, or dish variant.
- Do not add `llms.txt` only for AI SEO.
- Do not block important pages in robots.txt.
- Do not publish schema that is not visible on the page.
- Do not over-optimize headings with repeated city/location terms.
