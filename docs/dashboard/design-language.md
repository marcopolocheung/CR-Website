# Internal design language

The current authority is the [Night shift design bank](../design/internal-language/README.md), approved November 10, 2026: **Layered paper + Patina teal**, white/ink/public-site red, consistent outline icons and the moving red navigation highlight.

Read the [actual design guidelines](../design/internal-language/guidelines.md) and [interactive examples](../design/internal-language/reference.html?depth=paper&accent=patina) before changing an internal screen. Use the [implementation guide](../internal-tools-overhaul/implementation-guide.md) for staged overhaul work and verification.

The [October design-language document](design-language-2026-10.md) is retained as historical context. Its black-only primary buttons, red-only-for-danger rule and paper-mirroring screen layouts are superseded by the approved bank. Preserve business rules, recognizable field names and print outputs; entry screens may use the bank's focused workflows.
