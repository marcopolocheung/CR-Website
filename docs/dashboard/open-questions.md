# Open questions for the owner

Answers change what gets built. Scheduler-specific questions are in `AMBIGUITIES.md`.

## People and access
1. Who uses each tool today? (Owner only / store managers / shift leads.) Do shift leads do cash out themselves?
2. Is one shared manager code acceptable for now, or do you want each manager to have their own PIN so records show who saved them?
3. Should crew ever log in (to request time off or swap shifts), or does that stay by text message?

## Cash
4. What over/short amount is "worth looking into"? (Suggested: ignore under $1, flag over $5.)
5. Is the starting bank always $100 per drawer, at both stores?
6. Do cashiers see the Toast expected figure before they count? (Blind count question.)
7. How long do you need cash-out records kept? Does the accountant want a monthly file?
8. When a drawer is short, what happens today: conversation, write-up, deduction form? (Shapes how the screen links them.)

## Inventory
9. Is the clipboard sheet in the order items sit on the shelves? If not, what order would make the walk shortest?
10. Who are the suppliers behind the codes (D, L, LC, B, Pepsi), and how is each one ordered (phone, text, rep visit, website)? What are the order cutoffs?
11. What unit is each "Use" number in (case, sleeve, box)?
12. Should CR2 get the same inventory screen? Is there a CR2 paper sheet?

## Bookkeeping
13. Who receives the daily sales workbook, and how (printed, emailed, shared drive)?
14. What's the pay period (weekly, biweekly) and its start day?

## Forms
15. Which forms are used most often? Which ones do you wish were easier?
16. Should customer complaints be logged on screen?
17. Has anyone reviewed the hiring application questions recently?

## General
18. What's the single most annoying part of running the stores' paperwork today? (Build that next.)
19. Would you consider paying for 7shifts or MarginEdge instead of building scheduling or inventory features? (See improvements.md #35.)
